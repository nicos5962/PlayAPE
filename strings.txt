# String file for PlayAPE plugin

PLUGIN_PLAYAPE
	EN	Play APE

PLUGIN_PLAYAPE_DESC
	EN	Play APE with embedded CUE support and scanner-safe registration

PLUGIN_PLAYAPE_EMBEDDED_CUE
	EN	Embedded cuesheet

PLUGIN_PLAYAPE_EMBEDDED_CUE_DESC
	EN	Split APE files that contain a CUESHEET tag into virtual tracks. A rescan is needed after changing this setting.
